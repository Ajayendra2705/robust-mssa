# Design v2 — experiment 3: forecasting arm

_Generated by `experiments/03_design_v2/run_forecast.py` in 416s; 216 rolling-origin runs, 3 seeds per cell._

## Protocol

Rolling origin, 4 origins, horizons h = 1…12, refit at every origin on the **contaminated** history, scored against the **clean** signal (so predicting an outlier earns no credit). Recurrent (M)SSA forecast: the LRR is read off the fitted subspace, so the robust cells forecast with a robust LRR applied to a robust reconstruction.

Panels: p=4 series, k=2 common factors, additive outliers at 8× sd.

## Note on the stability guard

51 of 864 origins (5.9%) produced an **explosive** linear recurrence and were refused rather than scored.

This is not a nuisance — it is a finding. A near-vertical estimated subspace makes the `1/(1 − ν²)` factor in the LRR enormous. On the shortest training window a robust fit on AirPassengers landed at ν² = 0.9971, giving a dominant characteristic root of 1.337 and a 12-step forecast ~140× the series' own scale; other seeds diverged to ~10⁸. Averaged in naively, a single such origin sets the cell's RMSE and hides every real effect in the table. Origins are therefore rejected when the dominant root implies growth beyond 10× over the horizon — a threshold that still permits genuine growth, since a trending series legitimately has a root just above 1.
